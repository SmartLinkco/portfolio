About Stephen Gyan Bimpong

Stephen Gyan Bimpong is a systems architect and public speaker based in Ghana. He is CEO of VeriTrack Systems, the company behind veritrack.cloud, a SaaS product for photo-verified, geofenced staff attendance. Through SmartLinkco he builds websites and systems for clients.

He has a computer science background and uses it on workforce systems, SaaS and web applications, fintech and payment integrations, messaging platforms, and data-driven business tools. The public projects on the home page are the concrete record of that work.

Coaching and speaking

Alongside the products, Stephen coaches individuals and teams. The coaching is built around real projects and how to think about a system, not a stack of tutorials. He also speaks in the rooms documented on this site, including GAEC, the ATF challenge, Korle Bu, DynamicsCon, Demo Friday, GHIE, and IEEE.

His work has included a nomination as Best Student Software Developer in Information Technology at the UMB Ghana Tertiary Awards, and he takes part in developer communities, innovation showcases, and buildathons.

The standard he uses is simple: a system should be intentional, able to scale, secure, and usable by the people who have to run it.

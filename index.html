The work

Systems organisations can run.

Stephen Gyan Bimpong designs them, ships them, and speaks about the work in public.

He is CEO of VeriTrack Systems. VeriTrack, at veritrack.cloud, is a Ghana SaaS product for photo-verified, geofenced staff attendance: a record of who was actually on site.

Through SmartLinkco he builds websites and systems for clients. The selected projects below are the public record of that kind of delivery, from attendance platforms to festival, finance, and community sites.

He also speaks and coaches. Engagements already documented on this site include GAEC, the ATF challenge, Korle Bu, DynamicsCon, Demo Friday, GHIE, IEEE, Africa Tech Summit, FinTech Week, TechJam, and Google for Startups.

The useful question is whether the system can be operated after the launch.
